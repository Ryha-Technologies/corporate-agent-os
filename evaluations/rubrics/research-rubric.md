# Research rubric

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | Evidence to cite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source quality | No sources or fabricated | Leads and SEO pages only | Mostly primary/strong with gaps | Primary and strong sources, dated, accessible | Ledger rows opened |
| Traceability | Claims unsourced | Some claims sourced | Most consequential claims sourced | Every consequential claim has a ledger id | Ledger coverage check |
| Disconfirming evidence | None sought | Mentioned | Recorded for key findings | Recorded and weighed, changed conclusions where warranted | Brief section |
| Labelling | Facts and guesses mixed | Partial labelling | Observation/finding/interpretation/hypothesis separated | Separated with confidence reasons | Brief |
| Market sizing | Single number or none | Top-down only | Bottom-up with formula | Bottom-up, sourced inputs, low/base/high, sensitivity | Sizing table |
| Decision usefulness | Does not answer the decision | Answers vaguely | Answers with confidence | Answers, states what would change it and the next experiment | Brief |
