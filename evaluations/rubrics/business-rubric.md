# Business rubric

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | Evidence to cite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem framing | Solution-first, no person | Person named, cost vague | Person, situation, workaround, cost | Multiple framings compared with evidence grades | Problem statements |
| Evidence grading | None | Implicit | Per pillar | Per pillar with ids and confidence | Assessment table |
| Business model reasoning | Single model asserted | Options listed | Options compared on margin and payback | Compared with ranges, variable AI/cloud costs, sensitivity | Business model document |
| Differentiation | Feature list | Adjectives | Mechanism named | Mechanism with evidence and incumbent response | Strategy memo |
| Kill criteria | None | Vague | Numeric or observable | Numeric, dated, with experiments and pivots | Assessment |
| Honesty | Promises outcomes | Optimistic framing | Estimates labelled | Estimates labelled, "stop" considered, professional review listed | Assessment |
