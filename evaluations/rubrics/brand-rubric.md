# Brand rubric

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | Evidence to cite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Research before creation | None | Generic category notes | Category audit with references | Audit with dated references, clichés and ownable signals | Category audit |
| Direction diversity | One direction | Recolours of one idea | Two distinct ideas | Three or more distinct strategic ideas compared, rejected recorded | Direction comparison |
| Fact/framing separation | Invented facts | Mixed | Separated | Separated per sentence, proof points verified | Brand strategy |
| Naming screening | None or clearance claim | Web search only | Databases per jurisdiction | All jurisdictions and classes, variants, statuses, limitations | Naming clearance records |
| Identity craft | Only works with effects | Works large only | Works small and mono | Full variant set, specs, tested contexts | Identity concepts |
| Rights and provenance | None | Partial | Most assets recorded | Every asset recorded with licence evidence | Asset provenance |
| Distinctiveness | Resembles a competitor | Category default | Different in places | Recognisably different with documented comparison | Pattern-map comparison |
