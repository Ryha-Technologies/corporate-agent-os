# Originality scorecard

Used by `originality-audit` and the brand, design and research reviewers. Score 0–3 per dimension only where evidence exists; otherwise `NOT_ASSESSABLE` with the missing evidence named. A deliverable fails the originality check if any dimension scores 0, or if two or more score 1, or if "Evidence quality" scores below 2.

| # | Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research depth | No research before creation | Generic notes | Category/competitor audit with references | Audit with dated references, clichés, ownable signals, pattern map | Category audit, pattern map |
| 2 | Relevance | Does not address the brief or audience | Loosely related | Addresses the brief | Addresses the brief with audience evidence | Brief, research ids |
| 3 | Specificity | Could describe any company | Some specifics | Specific to this product and audience | Specific, concrete, uses customer language and real content | Deliverable text |
| 4 | Differentiation | Imitates a competitor or template | Category default | Different in places | Recognisably different; comparison documented; no distinctive-asset borrowing | Pattern-map comparison |
| 5 | Strategic coherence | Contradicts strategy | Loosely connected | Consistent | Every decision traces to strategy or research ids | Rationale document |
| 6 | Practical usefulness | Cannot be acted on | Needs heavy interpretation | Actionable | Actionable with specs, acceptance criteria or next steps | Deliverable |
| 7 | Craftsmanship | Placeholders, errors, broken output | Rough | Clean | Clean, tested in its medium (rendered, read, operated) | Review record |
| 8 | Evidence quality | Fabricated or none | Unsourced claims | Mostly sourced | Every claim sourced or labelled; no invented facts | Ledger ids |
| 9 | Consistency | Contradicts other artefacts | Terminology drifts | Consistent | Consistent across PRD, brand, UI, docs and code | Cross-artefact check |
| 10 | Audience suitability | Wrong audience | Partly suitable | Suitable | Suitable with evidence of audience expectations and accessibility | Research, accessibility notes |

## Corrective actions by failing dimension

| Dimension failing | Required action |
|---|---|
| Research depth | Produce the category audit and pattern map before continuing. |
| Specificity | Rewrite generic passages with customer language and evidence ids; cut what cannot be made specific. |
| Differentiation | Return to directions; generate new strategic ideas from the pattern-map gaps; record rejections. |
| Evidence quality | Source or remove each claim; convert unsupported statements to labelled hypotheses. |
| Craftsmanship | Fix defects; inspect in the final medium; re-review. |
| Consistency | Align terminology to the glossary; reconcile contradictions and record the decision. |

## Limits

Similarity checks, including any automated or embedding-based comparison, are signals for human review. They do not prove originality, nor infringement. Legal questions go to counsel.
