# Trademark search starting points

Public databases for preliminary screening. None of these searches is legal clearance. Registers change daily, unregistered (common-law) rights exist in many jurisdictions, and similarity is judged on sound, appearance, meaning and the relatedness of goods/services, not exact spelling. Record URL and access date for every search.

| Jurisdiction | Database | URL | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| International (many registers) | WIPO Global Brand Database | https://branddb.wipo.int/ | Aggregates national and international (Madrid) records; coverage and update lag vary by office. Supports image search for device marks. |
| India | IP India Trade Marks public search | https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/ | Search by wordmark, phonetic and Vienna code. Check status (Registered, Objected, Opposed, Abandoned). Classes follow Nice. |
| United States | USPTO Trademark Search | https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search | Search live and dead marks; dead marks can still indicate prior common-law use. Check goods/services and status. |
| European Union | EUIPO eSearch plus | https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/ | EU trade marks; also check national registers of key member states. |
| EU and partner offices | TMview | https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/ | Federated search across many national offices. |
| United Kingdom | UK IPO search | https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark | Separate from EU since 2021. |
| Nice classification | WIPO Nice Classification | https://www.wipo.int/classifications/nice/ | Identify the classes for the actual goods/services and the adjacent classes a reviewer would compare. |

## Search coverage checklist per candidate

- Exact mark; with and without spaces; plural; common misspellings
- Phonetic equivalents (k/c, ph/f, i/y, double letters)
- Transliterations in audience scripts (e.g. Devanagari, Tamil) where relevant
- Translations and meanings in audience languages
- Dominant element alone (for compound names)
- Relevant Nice classes plus adjacent classes (e.g. software 9, SaaS 42, business services 35, finance 36)
- Device/logo similarity where a mark is proposed (Vienna codes, image search)
- Company registries (e.g. MCA India, state registries US, national registries EU)
- Domains (.com, .in, .eu, country and category TLDs), app stores, major social handles, package registries
- General web and marketplace use for unregistered rights

## Limitations to state in every report

- Databases lag filings; a mark filed last week may not appear.
- Common-law and unregistered use is not in registers.
- Likelihood-of-confusion analysis depends on jurisdiction-specific law and is for counsel.
- Automated similarity (including embeddings) is a signal for human review, not a result.
