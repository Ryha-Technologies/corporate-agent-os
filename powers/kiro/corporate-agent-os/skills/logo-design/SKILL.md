# Logo and symbol design

## Purpose
Create a mark that expresses the brand's strategic idea, works as a simple shape in ordinary use, and comes with the specifications and rights records a production team needs.

## When to use
- A brand strategy and a shortlisted name exist and need a visual mark.
- An existing logo fails at small sizes, in monochrome or in distinctiveness.

## Preconditions
- **Research before design (mandatory).** Follow `skills/ui-ux-design/references/design-research-method.md` steps 1 to 4 before producing any mark, wordmark or logo system: the job statement; a dated category research table (at least three direct competitors, two adjacent, one non-digital reference) with a pattern map of the category template; the psychology analysis from `skills/ui-ux-design/references/layout-psychology.md` with the concrete implication of each chosen principle; and at least three genuinely different directions scored against job fit, uniqueness (swap test), psychology, accessibility and cost. Present the comparison for approval. The first idea is never built.
- Brand strategy, name candidate(s) with screening status, category visual audit.
- Access to vector tooling or a designer who will produce vectors from the specification.

## Inputs
- Brand strategy, personality, audience, usage contexts (app icon, web header, print, signage, social avatar).
- Competitor pattern map (visual section).

## Procedure
1. **Start from strategy**, not from "a cool icon": name meaning, audience, competitors, usage contexts.
2. **Visual audit.** Collect competitor and category marks with sources; list overused symbols, motifs and styles to avoid.
3. **Concept territories.** Explore at least three genuinely different territories, each expressing a strategic idea. Different colour or rotation of the same icon is one territory, not three.
4. **Craft.** Strong silhouette, legibility, balanced negative space, memorable geometry, typographic character matching the personality. Remove detail that collapses at small sizes.
5. **Tests.** Black and white before colour. Favicon (16 px), app icon, social avatar, header, document footer, signage, print. Optical balance, spacing, kerning, alignment, geometry, clear space; correct optically, not only mathematically.
6. **Variants.** Primary, symbol, wordmark, horizontal, stacked, monochrome, reverse, small-size versions as needed; minimum sizes and clear space.
7. **Delivery.** Editable vector source (SVG/PDF/AI or equivalent), transparent PNG exports, colour and type specifications, asset naming convention.
8. **Rights and screening.** Record origin and licence of every font, icon, vector element, reference and generated asset. Never trace or reproduce another company's mark, mascot or protected artwork. Run a preliminary design-mark similarity screen (`naming-ip-screen/references/trademark-offices.md`) and record its limitations.
9. **Compare and record.** Score territories on the originality scorecard; record rejected concepts with reasons.

## Outputs
- `03-brand/visual-audit.md`, `03-brand/identity-concepts.md`, `03-brand/assets/logo/**`, `03-brand/assets/asset-provenance.md`
- Input to `brandbook-identity`.

## Quality criteria
- A written research record (job, category table and pattern map, psychology analysis, three scored directions, approved choice) exists and is attached; the result passes the swap test against three competitors and has zero unexplained hits on `evaluations/originality/slop-patterns.md`.
- Works as a simple mark in black and white at 16 px and at signage scale.
- Three or more distinct territories compared; rejected concepts recorded.
- Every asset has a recorded licence and origin.
- No reliance on gradients, shadows, mockup lighting or an explanatory paragraph to work.
- Design-mark screen performed and limitations stated; no uniqueness claim.

## Failure handling
- Mark only works with effects → simplify or change concept.
- Similarity found → change concept; record as rejected with the conflicting reference.
- No vector tooling → deliver precise construction specifications and a reference sketch; state that vectors are pending.

## Safety boundaries
- No tracing, remixing or near-copies of protected marks or artwork.
- Generated imagery does not establish originality or commercial-use rights; record the generator's terms.
- No "unique" or clearance claims without counsel.
