# Business case standard

Applies to opportunity assessments, strategy memos, business models and financial models.

| # | Requirement | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Problem statements name the person, situation, workaround and cost; observed evidence and hypotheses are separated. | Problem statements document |
| B2 | Market size is bottom-up with formula, inputs, sources and low/base/high; top-down figures only as a sanity check with methodology read. | Sizing table |
| B3 | Every financial input is a ranged assumption with a source or reasoning and a confidence; outputs are ranges. | Assumption register |
| B4 | Gross margin includes variable compute, storage, bandwidth, AI tokens, third-party APIs, payment fees and support. | Model |
| B5 | Payback, LTV and break-even are reported per scenario; LTV's churn assumption is evidenced or marked low confidence. | Results table |
| B6 | Sensitivity names the three most influential assumptions. | Sensitivity section |
| B7 | Differentiation is a mechanism (workflow, data, distribution, trust, economics, speed, interoperability, service, network effects, expertise) with evidence; feature-list differences are not differentiation. | Strategy memo |
| B8 | Kill criteria are numeric or observable with decision dates; pivot options are listed. | Opportunity assessment |
| B9 | The recommendation may be "stop"; no document assumes the idea proceeds. | Reviewer |
| B10 | No promises of outcomes, valuations or growth rates. Estimates are labelled ESTIMATE. | Reviewer |
| B11 | Pricing has no "unlimited" plan without a fair-use and cost policy; price anchored to value and alternatives. | Pricing checks |
| B12 | Tax, accounting and securities questions are listed for qualified professionals, not answered. | Professional review section |
