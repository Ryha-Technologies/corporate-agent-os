# Legal research standard

Applies to naming screens, licensing reviews, privacy analyses, issue registers and any artefact touching legal exposure. **Every artefact under this standard is preliminary research, not legal advice.**

| # | Requirement | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Every artefact states that it is preliminary research and that qualified counsel in the named jurisdiction is required for conclusions. | Reviewer |
| L2 | Questions are stated with jurisdiction, dated official source, and whether the source is enacted law, draft, regulator guidance, standard or vendor statement. | Issue register |
| L3 | No statement that a name, mark, asset, product or process is "cleared", "safe", "compliant" or "available". Allowed naming statuses only: NOT_SCREENED, PRELIMINARY_LOW_CONFLICT_SIGNAL, POTENTIAL_CONFLICT, INSUFFICIENT_DATA, COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Violation is a critical gate failure. | Reviewer |
| L4 | Trademark screening covers every target jurisdiction, relevant and adjacent Nice classes, exact, phonetic, spelling and transliteration variants, company registries, domains, handles and unregistered use; limitations are listed. | Naming clearance record |
| L5 | Every third-party component has an SPDX identifier (or NOASSERTION flagged), licence text read, use mode analysed and obligations recorded; copyleft is analysed by use mode, not auto-rejected; unknown licences block. | Component register |
| L6 | Every font, image, icon, illustration, model, dataset and generated asset has a provenance record with licence evidence and permitted use. | Asset provenance |
| L7 | Privacy analysis covers data inventory, purpose, minimisation, retention, rights, processors, transfers and breach process; consent banners are not treated as lawful handling. | Privacy analysis |
| L8 | Advertising and product claims are substantiated or removed; comparative claims reference evidence. | Messaging review |
| L9 | Agents never file, sign, accept terms, purchase, register or contact regulators. | Permissions policy |
| L10 | Unresolved questions are routed to counsel with priority and deadline; they are never closed as acceptable by an agent. | Counsel questions list |
