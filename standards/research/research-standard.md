# Research standard

Applies to every research artefact (research brief, evidence ledger, competitor map, customer findings, feasibility memo).

## Requirements

| # | Requirement | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | The decision the research informs is written before searching. | Brief header |
| R2 | Every consequential claim has an evidence-ledger row with publisher, URL, published and access dates, source type, method, limitation and confidence. | Auditor opens a sample |
| R3 | Source hierarchy applied: primary > peer-reviewed/audited > named-source analysis > community (qualitative only) > leads (never sole support). | Ledger source-type column |
| R4 | Disconfirming evidence was actively sought and recorded for each key finding. | Brief section |
| R5 | Statements are labelled observation, finding, interpretation, hypothesis or recommendation; never mixed in one sentence. | Reviewer reading |
| R6 | Market and cost figures show formula, inputs, sources and low/base/high. | Sizing table |
| R7 | Sample size, sampling bias, survivorship, recency, geography and conflicts of interest are stated. | Limitation column |
| R8 | Unreachable or paywalled sources are marked UNVERIFIED, not paraphrased from memory. | Ledger |
| R9 | No fabricated sources, statistics, quotes, interviews or competitors. Violation is a critical gate failure. | Auditor |
| R10 | Customer research reports only what recorded sessions or supplied data show; simulated personas are labelled HYPOTHESIS. | Findings document |

## Confidence labels
- **High**: two or more independent primary or strong sources agree; method matches the question; recent.
- **Medium**: one strong source or several weaker ones; some method mismatch or age.
- **Low**: single weak source, inference, or stated intent only.
