# Brand standard

Applies to brand strategy, naming, identity, brandbooks and creative output.

| # | Requirement | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| BR1 | A category audit with dated references and a cliché list exists before any concept. | Category audit document |
| BR2 | At least three genuinely different directions (distinct strategic ideas, not recolours) are compared on the originality scorecard; rejected concepts are recorded. | Direction comparison, rejected-concepts record |
| BR3 | Narrative sentences are marked verified fact or brand framing; no invented founder history, testimonials, traction, awards or partnerships. | Brand strategy document |
| BR4 | Values are written as observable behaviours. | Brand strategy |
| BR5 | Every claim in messaging has a proof point or an evidence task; prohibited claims are listed. | Messaging document |
| BR6 | Names have territory logic, linguistic screening in audience languages and preliminary trademark screening per target jurisdiction with allowed statuses only. | Naming report |
| BR7 | No clearance language ("trademark safe", "copyright free", "available worldwide", "legally cleared") anywhere. Violation is a critical gate failure. | Reviewer |
| BR8 | Marks work in black and white at 16 px and at signage scale without effects. | Identity concepts |
| BR9 | Every font, image, icon, illustration, vector element and generated asset has a provenance row with licence and permitted use. | Asset provenance record |
| BR10 | The identity system is compared against the competitor pattern map and the differences are documented; one or two repeatable brand devices are chosen. | Brandbook distinctiveness section |
| BR11 | Colour pairings used for text and controls meet the accessibility target with measured ratios. | Brandbook colour section |
| BR12 | Default AI aesthetics (arbitrary gradients, glowing purple/blue, glass cards, oversized rounded rectangles, 3D blobs, template hero sections) carry a documented brand reason or are removed. | Reviewer |
