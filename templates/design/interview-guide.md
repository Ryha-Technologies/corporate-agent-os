## Script (non-leading, about recent real behaviour)
1. Tell me about the last time <problem> happened.
2. What triggered it?
3. What did you do? What did you do instead of a tool?
4. How often does this occur?
5. What did it cost you in time, money, risk or frustration?
6. Who else was involved in deciding or doing?
7. What have you already paid for or tried? What happened?
8. If this disappeared tomorrow, what would change for you?

Avoid: "Would you use…?", "Do you like…?", describing our solution before the end.
