# Validation plan

Filled by: business-strategist / product-manager. Reviewed by: business-model-auditor. Each experiment is pre-registered before it runs.

| # | Assumption tested (A-/H- id) | Experiment type (interview / landing page with commitment / concierge / prototype test / pre-sale / survey) | Audience and recruiting | Sample or duration | Metric | Decision rule (continue / pivot / stop thresholds) | Cost | Owner | Date | Result (filled after) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules
- The decision rule is written before the experiment starts and is not changed after seeing results.
- Stated intent ("would you use it?") is weaker than behaviour (sign-up with payment details, time spent, repeat use); prefer behaviour.
- Results are recorded with sample size, bias and confidence, then the ledger entry for the assumption is updated or withdrawn.
