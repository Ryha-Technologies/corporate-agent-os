# Owner answers

Copy to `00-intake/owner-answers.md` in the project before the audit phase. The orchestrator reads it instead of asking, and the proposal stops blocking on these. Answer in your own words. Write `UNKNOWN` where you do not know; that is a valid answer and is recorded as an assumption.

## Ownership and name
- Who owns the code, the name and the domain (person, company, shared)?
- May the name and brand be changed, or must they stay? If they may change, any names you already like or rule out?
- Anyone whose permission is needed (co-owners, employer, clients)?

## What it is for
- In one sentence, who uses the product and what do they get done with it?
- Who pays today, how much, how often? If nobody, say so.
- What must not change (features, URLs, data, behaviour, stack)?

## Earning
- How should it earn: subscriptions, one-time sales, services, ads, sponsorship, marketplace fee, other?
- Countries you sell to and countries you are paid in. Payment methods your customers actually use.
- Do you have a payment provider, bank account or tax registration already?

## Limits
- Budget for the whole job (money and your hours per week) and the date you want to be live.
- Risk you accept (for example: may the live site go down for a few minutes during a release?).
- Tools you must use or must not use (hosting, frameworks, libraries, fonts).

## Taste and evidence
- Three products or sites whose look you admire, and one thing about each. Three you dislike, and why.
- Any customers, support messages or analytics you can share (paste or point to files).

## Approvals you want to keep
- Which actions need your explicit yes besides the defaults (merge, deploy, payments live, publishing, spending, deleting)?
