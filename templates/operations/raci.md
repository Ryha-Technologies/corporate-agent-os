# RACI / DACI for <decision or deliverable>

R = responsible (does the work) · A = accountable (one person) · C = consulted · I = informed. For DACI, D = driver, A = approver, C = contributors, I = informed.

| Activity or decision | Role 1 | Role 2 | Role 3 | Role 4 | Agents involved (never A) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules: exactly one A per row; agents may be R-assist under a human R, never A; approvals recorded in 10-decisions/approvals.md.
