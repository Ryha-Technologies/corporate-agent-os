# Sales and partnerships

## Purpose
Create a repeatable way to find customers who need the product, sell what exists, forecast honestly and partner without dangerous dependency.

## When to use
- Before the first sales conversations; when forecasts are unreliable; when a partnership is proposed.

## Preconditions
- Positioning, pricing hypotheses and verified product capabilities.

## Inputs
- Strategy memo, ICP hypotheses, pricing, product truth (shipped vs roadmap), legal templates.

## Procedure
1. **ICP.** Firmographics and behaviours only where they predict need, buying process and value. Identify buyer, user, champion, blocker, procurement and technical approver.
2. **Qualification framework.** Problem urgency, current alternatives, budget, authority, decision process, security and compliance needs, timeline, measurable value.
3. **Discovery questions** about real workflows, cost of inaction, constraints, success measures. Never oversell unshipped capability.
4. **Pipeline.** Stage definitions with exit criteria, owner, next action and date, value range, probability rationale, source, close criteria, risk. Forecasts come from stage evidence, not optimism.
5. **Proposals.** Customer problem, scope, outcomes, assumptions, exclusions, implementation, service levels, security and privacy, pricing, payment terms, change control.
6. **Partners.** Evaluate audience overlap, distribution, trust, technical fit, incentives, support burden, exclusivity risk, data sharing, exit terms.
7. **Contracts.** Counsel review for liability, indemnity, IP, data processing, exclusivity, auto-renewal; agents never accept terms.
8. **Handoff** to customer success with goals, promises made and open items.

## Outputs
- `09-operations/sales-playbook.md`, `09-operations/pipeline-stages.md`, `09-operations/proposal-template.md` instance, `09-operations/partner-evaluation.md`.

## Quality criteria
- ICP attributes are predictive, with the evidence noted.
- Stage probabilities have rationale.
- Proposals state exclusions and assumptions.
- Partner evaluations cover exit and data terms.

## Failure handling
- No pipeline data → start with hypotheses and mark probabilities as unvalidated.
- Customer requests unshipped features → record as roadmap input; never promise.

## Safety boundaries
- Pricing, discounts, terms and signatures require human approval and counsel where needed.
- No unlimited liability or exclusivity without counsel.
