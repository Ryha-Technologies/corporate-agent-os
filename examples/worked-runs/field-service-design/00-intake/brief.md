# Project brief

Filled by: corporate-orchestrator. Date: 2026-11-18.

## Request as stated
"Use the system to design the field-service scheduling tool for Indian SMEs (plumbers, electricians, AC repair, 5–50 technicians) who currently coordinate jobs over WhatsApp." (user, this session; product option a)

## Problem
(F) The user wants a design for a scheduling/dispatch tool for small field-service businesses in India. (A-001) Owners or a dispatcher assign jobs to technicians over WhatsApp and phone. (A-002) This causes missed jobs, double bookings and no record of who did what. (A-003) Technicians use low- to mid-range Android phones in the field, often with patchy connectivity.

## Affected users and buyers
(A-004) Primary users: dispatcher/owner (desktop or phone, office or on the move) and technician (phone, in the field). Buyer: owner. Secondary: customer receiving status messages (off-platform, via WhatsApp/SMS). Affected non-users: accountant receiving job records.

## Current workaround
(A-005) WhatsApp groups, phone calls, a paper diary or spreadsheet.

## Desired outcome and definition of success
(A-006) A dispatcher can assign or reassign a job in under 20 seconds on a phone; a technician can see today's jobs and mark one complete in under 10 seconds; no job is lost. Metrics to be set in a PRD; none exist yet.

## Constraints
| Constraint | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Geography and languages | India; English UI first, Hindi and one regional language planned | A-007 |
| Platform | Mobile web first (Android browsers), desktop web for dispatch board | A-008 |
| Data sensitivity | Customer names, phone numbers, addresses (personal data) | F-002 (inherent) |
| Brand | None exists; neutral tokens; name unscreened | F-003 |
| Budget / timeline | Not stated | — |

## Chosen workflow
`product-design` (D-001). Prerequisites missing: PRD, brandbook, competitor pattern map, customer research. Recorded as Q-001..Q-003 and carried as HYPOTHESIS inputs.
