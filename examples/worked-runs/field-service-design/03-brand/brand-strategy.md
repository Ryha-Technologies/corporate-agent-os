# Brand strategy

Role: brand-strategist (T-013). Reviewed: brand-originality-auditor (see gate). Facts (F) vs framing (framing) marked per sentence.

## Audience and tension
Small field-service owners and dispatchers in India (F-001). Tension (HYPOTHESIS A-002): every missed or double-booked job is a lost customer and an argument with a technician; the owner carries the whole day in their head and in WhatsApp threads.

## Frame of reference and positioning
Frame: job scheduling for small service teams (not "FSM platform"). Position: the tool that makes sure nothing falls through the day — for teams of 5–50 who run on WhatsApp today. Alternatives: WhatsApp groups and a diary; heavier FSM suites (pattern map).

## Value
| Functional | Emotional | Social |
|---|---|---|
| Every job has a technician, a time and a status | The owner stops carrying the day in their head | Customers see a business that keeps its word |

## Reason to believe and proof points
All proof points are **to be earned**; no customers exist (F-003 context). Candidate proofs once live: jobs completed without reassignment, average time to assign, no-answer rate.

## Promise and boundaries
Promise (framing): "Nothing falls through." Boundaries: never claims AI, automation or "all-in-one"; never promises revenue growth.

## Personality and values (behaviours)
| Trait / value | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| Steady | Same place, same words, every day; no surprises in the UI | Dull marketing copy |
| Plain-spoken | Jobs, technicians, customers; Hindi and regional words where they are the real words | Jargon or English-only |
| Attentive | Surfaces what needs attention first | Nagging notifications |
| Respectful of the technician | Big targets, few taps, offline tolerance | Surveillance framing ("track your workers") |
