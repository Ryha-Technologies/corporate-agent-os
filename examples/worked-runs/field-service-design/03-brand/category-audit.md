# Category audit

Role: brand-strategist (T-013). Sources: `01-research/competitor-pattern-map.md` and `competitor-capture.json` (accessed 2026-11-18).

## Conventions (expected; keep unless research says otherwise)
- Status colours for job states; board or calendar for dispatch; mobile app for technicians; demo-led sales for mid-market, self-serve trial for small teams (Housecall Pro, FieldVibe).

## Clichés (observed in the set; avoid)
1. "AI operating system / AI-native" headlines (3 of 7).
2. "All-in-one" / "everything to run your business" (3 of 7).
3. Orange or electric-blue accent on navy (5 of 7).
4. Geometric sans (Inter/Poppins/Manrope family) (5 of 7).
5. "Field" + word names (5 of 9 in the wider set).
6. Hero screenshot of a dashboard with charts (observed on Zuper, Housecall Pro, FieldCamp pages during capture; not quantified).

## Trust signals used
Integration counts ("60+ Integrations", Zuper), testimonials (Housecall Pro), "built for solo pros" positioning (FieldVibe), YC badge (Fieldproxy).

## Customer language (HYPOTHESIS until interviews)
Owners say "jobs", "technician/boy/engineer", "visit", "customer didn't pick up", "double booking", "who is free". They do not say "work order", "resource", "dispatch optimisation".

## Ownable territory
A brand that speaks the dispatcher's language, looks like a well-kept whiteboard (ink + marker) rather than a SaaS dashboard, and is typeset in an Indian multi-script family. Verbal: concrete, unhurried, no "AI" in the headline.
