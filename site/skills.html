40 Agent Skills

Each skill is a folder with a SKILL.md file. The descriptions below are the first sentence of each skill's own description field. The grouping into areas is this site's organisation, not a field in the repository.

Orchestration and quality (5)

  • corporate-orchestrator

    Turn a rough product, company, service or application idea into an evidence-backed, reviewed delivery plan by coordinating specialist agents through a task DAG with quality gates.

  • anti-slop-quality-gate

    Self-check any AI-generated research, writing, code, UI, brand, image brief, deck or deliverable for generic patterns, fabricated claims, template visuals, placeholders, inconsistency and unverified functionality before submitting it for review.

  • originality-audit

    Independently audit a strategic or creative deliverable (research, business idea, name, brand, logo, UI, marketing, presentation, documentation, product strategy) for generic phrasing, template patterns, competitor imitation, unsupported claims, missing research and weak differentiation using the originality scorecard, and prescribe specific improvements.

  • qa-evaluation

    Independently verify a delivered artefact (requirements, UX, software, AI behaviour, documentation, release readiness) against the original request and acceptance criteria with reproducible tests and adversarial review, and issue GO / GO_WITH_ACCEPTED_RISKS / NO_GO.

  • test-strategy

    Derive a risk-based test strategy from the PRD, architecture and threat model; plan unit, integration, contract, end-to-end, regression, performance, accessibility, security and migration tests; build the traceability matrix and record results honestly.

Research and strategy (5)

  • research-intelligence

    Conduct rigorous market, customer, competitor, technology and scientific desk research with a decision frame, source hierarchy, evidence ledger, triangulation and disconfirming evidence.

  • competitive-analysis

    Map direct competitors, indirect alternatives, substitutes and manual workarounds with sourced pricing, distribution, switching costs and positioning gaps, and produce the competitor pattern map that brand and design.

  • business-idea-validation

    Investigate whether a rough or generic business idea is worth pursuing before any plan is written, by discovering the real problem, grading the evidence, assessing the opportunity, comparing business models, defining validation experiments, kill criteria and an MVP proposal.

  • strategy-market-sizing

    Develop a differentiated company or product strategy with a one-sentence strategic choice, behavioural segmentation, bottom-up market sizing, differentiation basis, wedge, KPIs, kill thresholds and a pre-mortem.

  • ux-research

    Plan, run and synthesise user interviews, surveys, usability tests and journey mapping into evidence-based personas, jobs-to-be-done, severity-ranked findings and design requirements, with simulated personas always labelled as hypotheses.

Product and design (5)

  • product-discovery-prd

    Translate customer evidence and a validated idea into a product thesis, user journeys, a PRD with testable Given/When/Then acceptance criteria, non-functional requirements, prioritised scope and release slices.

  • information-architecture

    Design navigation hierarchy, content models, page hierarchy, task flows and interaction models from user goals and content relationships, including error, empty, loading and responsive behaviour.

  • ui-ux-design

    Design distinctive, production-grade web or mobile interfaces from flows and IA: intentional visual direction per product category, all screen states, responsive and motion specs, accessibility notes and anti-template controls, plus implementation review against rendered UI.

  • design-system

    Define design principles, machine-readable design tokens (colour roles, typography scale, spacing, radius, elevation, motion, focus, status), layout rules, breakpoints and component specifications with variants, states, usage and accessibility rules so designs are implemented consistently.

  • accessibility-audit

    Review designs and implemented interfaces against WCAG 2.2 Level AA with combined automated and manual checks (contrast, structure, labels, keyboard, focus, target size, motion, errors, status messages), recording per-criterion PASS/FAIL/NOT_TESTED with evidence and specific fixes.

Brand and creative (5)

  • brand-strategy-story

    Create a distinctive brand strategy, positioning, personality, promise, narrative in three lengths and a messaging system grounded in a category audit and audience research, with verified facts separated from brand framing.

  • naming-ip-screen

    Generate distinctive company or product names from strategic territories and run preliminary linguistic, domain, social-handle, company-registry and trademark screening across the user's target jurisdictions (India, US, EU and WIPO starting points) with an explicit status per candidate.

  • logo-design

    Develop original, scalable logo concepts from brand strategy through several distinct concept territories, small-size and monochrome tests, full variant set and production vector delivery with recorded asset rights and a preliminary design-mark screen.

  • brandbook-identity

    Build a complete, usable brandbook and visual identity system (logo usage, colour with contrast pairings, licensed typography, grid, imagery, iconography, motion, voice, UI tokens, applications, misuse, governance) that another designer or engineer can reproduce.

  • creative-production

    Produce differentiated, accurate creative deliverables across documents, presentations, web copy, campaigns, graphics, image and video briefs with intake, multiple directions, brand and rights gates, rendered-output review and recorded provenance.

Engineering, data and operations (7)

  • architecture-system-design

    Design the simplest secure, maintainable and cost-aware architecture that meets stated requirements: quality attributes, option comparison, system boundaries, data flow, trust boundaries, contracts, reliability, cost model and ADRs.

  • api-database-design

    Design API contracts (resources, auth, error semantics, idempotency, pagination, versioning, rate limits) and database schemas (entities, constraints, indexes justified by queries, migrations, retention, backups) from the architecture and data model.

  • software-engineering

    Implement application features in small, maintainable, secure, tested increments following repository conventions: inspect first, vertical slices, validation at boundaries, tests with negative cases, formatter/linter/type/test/build runs with recorded output, reviewed diffs.

  • code-review

    Independently review a diff for correctness, security, maintainability, test adequacy, scope discipline and secrets, re-running reported build and test commands where possible and returning severity-ranked findings with file and line evidence.

  • data-ai-systems

    Select, integrate, evaluate and operate data pipelines and AI model features with task definition first, deterministic alternatives considered, representative evaluation sets, data rights and privacy governance, structured outputs, constrained tools, cost and latency controls and monitoring.

  • devops-sre

    Prepare, deploy, monitor and operate applications reliably: environments, CI/CD with pinned dependencies and scanning, least-privilege credentials, observability, SLOs, backup and tested restore, staged rollout, rollback and release checklist, without unapproved production changes.

  • technical-documentation

    Write and verify user documentation, installation guides, API references, runbooks, release notes and knowledge base articles against the shipped product by executing every documented command, keeping terminology consistent and listing known limitations.

Security, legal and incidents (5)

  • security-privacy

    Perform defensive security and privacy-by-design review of applications, APIs, AI workflows and infrastructure against OWASP ASVS and recognised guidance: authentication, session, authorisation, validation, injection, secrets, cryptography, logging, supply chain, privacy data handling, with prioritised findings and retests.

  • threat-modeling

    Build and maintain a threat model (assets, trust boundaries, entry points, STRIDE or attack-tree threats, abuse cases, likelihood and impact, controls, detection, tests, residual risk) from architecture through implementation.

  • incident-response

    Run security and reliability incident response: severity classification, incident command, containment within approved runbooks, evidence preservation, factual stakeholder communication drafts, recovery verification, notification-obligation flags and blameless postmortems with root causes and actions.

  • legal-risk-governance

    Identify legal, regulatory, contractual, privacy, consumer-protection, IP and governance issues in technology products and company operations through sourced research by jurisdiction, producing an issue register and counsel questions without giving legal advice.

  • oss-reuse-license

    Find, evaluate and integrate open-source components safely with repository due diligence, licence analysis by.

Business, growth and people (7)

  • business-model-unit-economics

    Build and stress-test business models with transparent unit economics (pricing, gross margin including variable compute and AI costs, CAC, LTV, payback, churn, break-even, sensitivity) and low/base/high scenarios.

  • finance-operations

    Create transparent operating budgets, revenue and cash models, pricing hypotheses, approval matrices, vendor registers and compliance question lists for technology companies, with every figure an explicit ranged assumption and professional review flagged.

  • people-operations

    Plan lean organisation structures, role charters with decision rights, workload-based capacity, fair job-related hiring criteria, secure onboarding and offboarding, operating rituals and human ownership of agent-augmented work.

  • sales-partnerships

    Build a B2B sales and partnership operating process: ICP on predictive attributes, buyer roles, qualification framework, discovery questions about real workflows, pipeline stages with evidence-based probabilities, proposal structure, partner evaluation and counsel-reviewed terms.

  • growth-gtm

    Create evidence-based go-to-market and growth plans: ICP and buyer confirmation, beachhead channel on access and economics, funnel events separating vanity from activation and revenue, pre-registered experiments, honest launch assets, privacy-minimised analytics and stop/scale thresholds.

  • product-launch

    Plan and execute a product launch: readiness confirmation across gates, launch strategy and tiers, positioning and messaging, launch calendar, marketing and PR assets, sales enablement, support documentation, monitoring plan, rollback and post-launch review.

  • customer-success-support

    Design customer onboarding, support operations, knowledge base, issue taxonomy, escalation, customer health signals and feedback-to-roadmap loops for software products, with honest service levels and privacy protection.
