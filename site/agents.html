58 agents in five tiers

1 orchestrator, 8 executives, 10 department leads, 28 specialists and 11 independent reviewers. Names, tiers, autonomy levels and descriptions below come from the Kiro agent files in .kiro/agents/*.json.

Orchestrator ()

Plans the work, writes task contracts, delegates and integrates. It does not approve its own work.

  • corporate-orchestrator · autonomy: execute

    Leads multi-department work from a rough idea or product request; plans the DAG, delegates to specialists, enforces gates and integrates. Use for any substantial company or product task.

Executives (8)

Advise on direction, risk and spend. Autonomy is advise: they recommend, a human decides.

  • ceo-strategy · autonomy: advise

    Sets direction, arbitrates trade-offs between departments and decides go/no-go at the opportunity, product and release gates. Use when a cross-department decision is needed.

  • cfo-finance · autonomy: advise

    Owns financial model review, pricing economics, runway and spend approval policy. Use at the opportunity gate and before any pricing or spend decision.

  • ciso-security · autonomy: advise

    Owns security risk acceptance, testing authorisation and incident policy. Use at the security gate and whenever personal, payment or regulated data is involved.

  • clo-legal · autonomy: advise

    Owns legal, IP, licensing and privacy risk triage and decides what must go to qualified counsel. Use at the brand and legal gates.

  • cmo-marketing · autonomy: advise

    Owns brand, positioning and go-to-market direction. Use at the brand gate and before launch planning.

  • coo-operations · autonomy: advise

    Turns strategy into an operating plan, owns process, capacity, vendors and the operating cadence. Use when planning delivery, operations or cross-team dependencies.

  • cpo-product · autonomy: advise

    Owns product strategy, scope discipline and the definition of success. Use at product gates and when scope, priority or metrics are disputed.

  • cto-technology · autonomy: advise

    Owns technology direction, architecture oversight, build-vs-buy and technical risk acceptance. Use when technology decisions have cost, security or lock-in consequences.

Department leads (10)

Coordinate the specialists in one area and propose plans.

  • brand-creative-director · autonomy: propose

    Directs brand strategy, naming, identity and creative production; ensures research-before-creation and multiple directions. Use for any brand or creative stage.

  • design-lead · autonomy: propose

    Coordinates UX research, information architecture, interaction and visual design, design system and accessibility. Use for the experience stage.

  • engineering-lead · autonomy: propose

    Coordinates architecture, implementation, integration and release engineering; owns branch and integration strategy for parallel work. Use for build stages.

  • growth-lead · autonomy: propose

    Coordinates positioning, launch, channels, sales enablement and customer success planning. Use for launch and growth stages.

  • legal-lead · autonomy: propose

    Coordinates IP screening, licensing review, privacy analysis and the counsel question list. Use for naming, OSS and legal stages.

  • operations-lead · autonomy: propose

    Coordinates finance operations, people operations, procurement, vendor management and internal process. Use when the plan needs budgets, roles, vendors or operating cadence.

  • product-lead · autonomy: propose

    Coordinates product discovery, PRD, prioritisation and validation planning. Use when turning evidence into product scope.

  • qa-lead · autonomy: propose

    Plans test strategy and coordinates independent verification across functional, accessibility, performance and AI behaviour. Use when planning or staffing verification.

  • research-lead · autonomy: propose

    Plans and coordinates research tracks (customer, market, competitor, technology), owns the evidence ledger quality. Use when a discovery stage needs several research tasks.

  • security-lead · autonomy: propose

    Coordinates threat modelling, secure design review, security testing within authorised scope and remediation tracking. Use for security stages.

Specialists (28)

Do the work inside an assigned write scope.

  • accessibility-specialist · autonomy: propose

    Reviews designs and implementations against WCAG 2.2 AA with automated and manual checks, and specifies fixes. Use at design and testing stages.

  • backend-engineer · autonomy: propose

    Implements server-side features, APIs, data access and migrations with tests, following repository conventions and secure defaults. Use for backend build tasks.

  • brand-strategist · autonomy: propose

    Builds brand foundation, positioning, personality, promise, narrative and messaging system from a category audit. Use for brand strategy work before naming or identity.

  • business-strategist · autonomy: propose

    Develops positioning, segment choice, differentiation and the opportunity assessment with confidence levels and kill criteria. Use at the opportunity stage.

  • competitive-analyst · autonomy: propose

    Maps competitors, alternatives, substitutes and workarounds with sourced pricing, distribution, switching costs and positioning gaps. Use for competitor maps and differentiation.

  • creative-producer · autonomy: propose

    Produces documents, presentations, web copy, campaign assets and creative briefs for images or video, with brand, rights and verification gates. Use for any creative deliverable beyond product UI.

  • customer-researcher · autonomy: propose

    Plans and synthesises customer interviews, surveys and usability tests; builds evidence-based personas and journeys. Use when primary research with real users is planned or analysed.

  • customer-success-specialist · autonomy: propose

    Designs onboarding, support operations, knowledge base, issue taxonomy, escalation and feedback loops. Use for post-launch customer operations.

  • data-ai-engineer · autonomy: propose

    Designs data pipelines and AI features with evaluation sets, guardrails, cost controls and privacy; compares deterministic options first. Use when a feature involves data processing or model calls.

  • design-systems-engineer · autonomy: propose

    Defines design tokens, typography scale, spacing, component specifications with variants and states, and documentation. Use when a reusable system is needed for implementation.

  • devops-engineer · autonomy: propose

    Sets up CI/CD, environments, infrastructure, observability, backup and release processes with least privilege and rollback. Use for deployment and operations tasks.

  • financial-modeler · autonomy: propose

    Builds business model and unit-economics models with explicit assumptions, scenarios, sensitivity and break-even. Use when revenue, cost, pricing or runway figures are needed.

  • frontend-engineer · autonomy: propose

    Implements accessible, responsive user interfaces from design specs and tokens, with real states and browser-verified rendering. Use for UI build tasks.

  • growth-marketer · autonomy: propose

    Builds positioning, launch messaging, channel plans, experiments and analytics plans from verified product capability and research. Use for marketing and launch content.

  • identity-designer · autonomy: propose

    Develops logo concepts, identity system, colour, typography, imagery direction and the brandbook specification with distinctiveness and rights checks. Use for visual identity work.

  • legal-researcher · autonomy: propose

    Researches IP, licensing, privacy and regulatory questions against official sources and maintains the component register, asset provenance and issue register. Use for legal gate evidence.

  • naming-specialist · autonomy: propose

    Generates name territories and candidates, screens linguistics, domains, handles and runs preliminary trademark searches with explicit status per jurisdiction. Use before any name is used publicly.

  • operations-analyst · autonomy: propose

    Builds operating budgets, approval matrices, vendor registers and procurement evaluations. Use when the plan needs budgets, vendors or process definitions.

  • people-ops-specialist · autonomy: propose

    Designs lean organisation structures, role charters, hiring criteria, onboarding and operating cadence. Use when roles, capacity or team process are planned.

  • product-manager · autonomy: propose

    Writes the product thesis, PRD, user journeys, acceptance criteria and analytics plan from evidence. Use when requirements must be made testable.

  • research-analyst · autonomy: propose

    Runs desk research with primary-source triangulation, evidence ledger and disconfirming evidence for markets, customers and technology. Use for any research task that must be sourced.

  • sales-specialist · autonomy: propose

    Defines ICP, qualification, discovery questions, pipeline stages, proposals and partner evaluation. Use when a sales motion or partnership is planned.

  • security-engineer · autonomy: propose

    Performs threat modelling, secure design and code review, dependency and secret scanning, and authorised security testing with findings and retests. Use for security review tasks.

  • software-architect · autonomy: propose

    Designs system architecture, boundaries, data flow, trust boundaries, ADRs and cost model from requirements. Use before implementation of anything non-trivial.

  • technical-writer · autonomy: propose

    Writes user documentation, API references, runbooks and release notes verified against the shipped behaviour. Use when documentation must match the product.

  • test-engineer · autonomy: propose

    Designs and implements unit, integration, contract, end-to-end, performance and accessibility tests with traceability to acceptance criteria. Use for verification tasks.

  • ui-designer · autonomy: propose

    Creates the visual design direction, high-fidelity screens, responsive layouts and motion within the brand and design system. Use after flows and IA exist.

  • ux-designer · autonomy: propose

    Designs user flows, information architecture, wireframes, interaction models and all screen states from research and the PRD. Use before any visual design.

Independent reviewers (11)

Judge deliverables at quality gates. The creator of a deliverable never approves it.

  • architecture-reviewer · autonomy: advise

    Independently reviews architecture, ADRs, data and API contracts, threat and cost models for unjustified complexity and missing failure modes. Use at the architecture gate.

  • brand-originality-auditor · autonomy: advise

    Independently audits brand strategy, names, logos and identity for competitor imitation, generic narrative, invented facts, missing category research and clearance overclaims. Use at the brand gate.

  • business-model-auditor · autonomy: advise

    Independently audits opportunity assessments and financial models for hidden assumptions, false precision, missing costs and absent kill criteria. Use at the opportunity gate.

  • code-reviewer · autonomy: advise

    Independently reviews diffs for correctness, security, maintainability, tests, scope and secrets, and re-runs reported build and test commands. Use at the build gate.

  • design-qa-auditor · autonomy: advise

    Independently audits designs and rendered UI for hierarchy, brand consistency, state completeness, accessibility, responsiveness and template-likeness. Use at the design gate.

  • documentation-auditor · autonomy: advise

    Independently verifies documentation against shipped behaviour by executing documented commands and checking paths, features and limitations. Use at the documentation gate.

  • legal-licensing-reviewer · autonomy: advise

    Independently reviews component registers, asset provenance, naming and issue registers for unknown licences, unanalysed obligations and clearance overclaims. Use at the legal gate.

  • research-auditor · autonomy: advise

    Independently audits research artefacts for source quality, fabrication, confirmation bias, missing disconfirming evidence and unsupported market sizing. Use at the discovery gate.

  • security-auditor · autonomy: advise

    Independently audits threat models, security reviews, scan results and privacy analyses; verifies authorisation of any testing and the honesty of remediation claims. Use at the security gate.

  • test-auditor · autonomy: advise

    Independently audits test evidence, traceability and release readiness; converts no NOT_RUN into PASS and issues GO / GO_WITH_ACCEPTED_RISKS / NO_GO. Use at the testing and release gates.

  • ux-auditor · autonomy: advise

    Independently audits product requirements and user flows for usability, task completion, unsupported personas and ambiguous requirements. Use at the product gate and for flow reviews.

Rules the agents work under

  • One writer per path. Parallel code work goes into separate worktrees.
  • Delegation uses the host's real sub-agent feature. If it is unavailable, the work runs as sequential passes and says so.
  • Anything not executed is reported as NOT_RUN.
  • Human approval is required before production deployment, publishing, spending, legal or IP filings, customer communications, processing real personal data, deleting data or changing access.

These rules are from the project's workspace steering file. Full definitions: the agents/ directory.
